SCOPE Air Tracker

A live flight-radar app, built by a UAV operations specialist — for UAV pilots.

A personal project I built to solve a problem I was facing in my own drone operations: knowing what's in the airspace around me before and during a flight. SCOPE Air Tracker renders live ADS-B traffic as a radar sweep and lets you filter aircraft by altitude band — so you can isolate low-level traffic in your operating area and keep situational awareness for safer flights within designated airspace. Built with UAV pilots in mind. Global coverage, airport weather and METAR, military airports and aircraft, aircraft photos and route lookups, live trails, and a watchlist that alerts you when a tracked aircraft appears or lands. Live now in the browser, with a native app in development.

Survey NOTAM & weather dashboard

An automated pre-flight screen for a rolling survey schedule. It reads planned jobs and aircraft limits from a spreadsheet, screens the NATS UK NOTAM feed and hourly forecast against each site’s position, altitude and time window, and renders a self-contained dashboard with a traffic-light status per survey. UAV RAMS automation system

A browser-based operational safety application that generates site-specific UAV Risk Assessments and Method Statements from reusable hazard and control libraries, driven by conditional inputs about the operation. Risk scoring, revision control, an approval workflow, and print-ready PDF output — so a compliant RAMS for a new site is a few minutes of structured input rather than an afternoon of copy-paste.
