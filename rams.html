What it does

Your RAMS, without the copy-paste

Built for consultancies who write RAMS for several contractor clients and need each one to look right, score consistently and be easy to revise.

A workspace for every client

Each client has their own letterhead colour, contacts, nearest A&E, assembly point and PPE rules, so every RAMS goes out looking like it was written for them.

  • Client letterhead on every document
  • Emergency details filled in automatically
  • Switch clients from one menu

UK construction hazard library

Tick the activities: roof work, scaffold, MEWPs, towers, lifting, hot works, excavation, electrical, dust, asbestos and more. The matching hazards, controls, PPE and method statement steps are added for you.

  • References WAH Regs 2005, LOLER, PUWER, COSHH, CAR 2012, EAWR 1989
  • Site conditions: public, occupied buildings, overhead lines, traffic…
  • Add site-specific hazards to any RAMS

Scoring and approval you can defend

Likelihood × severity on a 1–5 scale, initial and residual. A RAMS can’t be marked approved while any residual risk is still High, and approval records who signed it off and when.

  • Draft → In review → Approved
  • Approver name and date on the document
  • Competence and plant inspections the job needs

Revision control and a register

Every RAMS gets a reference and revision number. New revisions keep the old one current until they’re approved, and the full history prints on the document.

  • Searchable register for all clients
  • Print or save as PDF (A4)
  • Copy an old job as the start of a new one
Add-on for retained clients

Kit & crew readiness

Give your retained contractor clients a go/no-go check for every job. They keep their crew cards (CSCS, IPAF, PASMA, SMSTS, first aid…) and kit inspections (LOLER, PAT, MOT…) up to date, and the tool checks the approved RAMS, the right competence for the whole job and in-date kit before anyone starts. You see every client’s status in one place.

How it works

Four steps per RAMS

01

Job details

Client, site, dates, principal contractor and scope.

02

Work activities

Tick what’s being done; competence and plant requirements follow.

03

Site & risks

Add site conditions, edit controls and residual scores.

04

Review & approve

PPE, emergency arrangements, method statement, sign-off.

The hazard library gives you a consistent, well-referenced starting point. It doesn’t replace your judgement: every RAMS is reviewed, tailored to the site and approved by your competent person before it’s issued.
